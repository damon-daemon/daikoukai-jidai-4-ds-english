@111

@112
Rec Room (Common > Deck Screen)\nA room where mates and sailors relax. A mate placed here makes sailors tire less easily and recovers the mate's own fatigue and spirit.\n\n

@113
Lumber Room (Common > Deck Screen)\nA room with lumber for repairing the ship.\nA mate placed here becomes the "Shipwright". Requires a mate with Survey 130 or more.\nWith a Shipwright, the ship is repaired at sea. Repairs at the Shipyard also cost less.\n\n

@114
Sickbay (Common > Deck Screen)\nA room for treating the sick.\nA mate placed here becomes the "Doctor". Requires a mate with Insight 150 or more.\nWith a Doctor, illness is less likely and sailors tire less easily. Items that cure illness also become usable.\n\n

@115
Galley (Common > Deck Screen)\nA room for cooking meals.\nA mate placed here becomes the "Head Cook". Requires a mate with Accuracy 70 or more.\nWith a Head Cook, food and water run down more slowly at sea. Sailors also tire less easily.\n\n

@116
Animal Pen (Common > Deck Screen)\nA room for raising livestock for food.\nA mate placed here becomes the "Keeper". Requires a mate with Vitality 130 or more.\nWith a Keeper, food is produced during the voyage.\n\n

@117
Chapel (Common > Deck Screen)\nA room where mates and sailors pray.\nA mate placed here becomes the "Missionary". Requires a mate with Diplomacy 160 or more.\nWith a Missionary, sailors tire less easily, and you can use "{Distribute Goods:<$2B>}" in the Plaza to start a {trend:<$19>}.\n[!] Some mates refuse to be placed in the Chapel because of their beliefs.\n\n

@118
Staff Room (Common > Deck Screen)\nA room where the adviser plans stratagems.\nA mate placed here becomes the "Adviser". Requires a mate with Scheming 120 or more.\nWhen the Adviser has an idea, you can use "{Plan a Scheme:<$10>}" in the Tavern. The Adviser may also foil enemy schemes before they happen, and gathers information on other factions when you stay at an inn.\n\n

@119
Purser's Office (Common > Deck Screen)\nA room where the fleet's accounts are kept.\nA mate placed here becomes the "Purser". Requires a mate with Finance 100 or more.\nWith a Purser, you may get discounts at the Market. If you pay double, you can also buy out a stock. You cannot sell at double the price, though.\n\n

@120

@121

Requires a mate with Diplomacy 100 or more.\nWith a First Mate, you can use "{Send Negotiation Letter:<$1B>}" at the Guild, and sailors tire less easily. In a battle against a port, the First Mate urges it to surrender.\n\n

@122

@123

@124

[!] If the Lookout has high Accuracy, he or she may snipe the enemy admiral in battle. If the shot succeeds, you can capture that ship.\n\n

@145
City / Port (Port Data)\nShows the size of the port. The commands available depend on its size.\n["City"] A large port.\n["Port"] A small port.\n["Village"] A place where you cannot land but can resupply.\nAn event may turn a village into a port.\n\n

@146
Culture Area (Port Data)\nThe culture area the port belongs to. Trade goods prices differ by culture area.\nThe building signs (available commands) change too.\n\n

@147
Status (Port Data)\nThe port's status raises or lowers prices.\n["Normal"] The usual state.\n["War"] Weapons rise, the rest fall.\n["Plague"] Food and medicine rise, the rest fall.\n["Famine"] Food rises, the rest fall.\n["Boom"] Prices rise overall.\n["Recession"] Prices fall overall.\n["Scarce"] Prices rise for the goods in short supply.\n\n

@148
Growth (Port Data)\nShows how developed the port is.\nIt rises when you use "{Invest:<$17>}" at the Market.\nWhen it is high, the port may gain more specialties.\nThe number of sailors you can recruit and the types of cannons you can equip also change.\n\n

@149
Armament (Port Data)\nShows how well armed the port is.\nIt rises when you use "{Fund Military:<$25>}" at the Palace or Governor's. The higher it is, the harder the port is to make surrender when attacked.\n\n
