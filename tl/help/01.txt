@52
Change Sails (Shipyard > Refit)\nYou can change the sails on the masts.\nSome ships limit which sails they can carry. Square sails raise speed in a tailwind; lateen (triangular) sails raise speed in a headwind.\nTopsails raise speed in a tailwind; staysails raise speed in a headwind.\n\n

@53

@54

@55
Extra Armor (Refit > Extra Gear)\nPlating that protects the ship from hits. It raises max durability but slightly lowers speed.\n\n

@56
Bow Gear (Refit > Extra Gear)\nYou can fit a bowsprit sail, a bow cannon or a ram.\n[Bowsprit Sail] Raises speed in a tailwind.\n[Bow Cannon] Fires forward.\n[Ram] Attacks enemy ships by ramming them.\n\n

@57
Stern Gear (Refit > Extra Gear)\nYou can fit a jigger spanker (stern sail) or a stern cannon.\n[Gaff Spanker] Raises speed in a headwind.\n[Aft Cannon] Fires backward.\n\n

@58
Change Cannons (Shipyard > Refit)\nChoose the cannon shown at the helm to change the cannon type.\nAs a port grows, up to 6 cannon types become available.\n\n

@59
Cargo Hold (Shipyard > Refit)\nYou can change what a {Cargo Hold:<$7F>} is used for: Marine Quarters, Armed Battery, Rowers' Room, Supply Hold or Cargo Store. Rowers' Rooms cannot be changed or added.\nSome holds cannot be set to certain uses. Adding a hold slightly lowers speed. One ship can have up to 4 Marine Quarters and Armed Batteries, up to 5 Cargo Stores, and any number of Supply Holds.\n\n

@60
Rooms (Shipyard > Refit)\nYou can change {Rooms:<$6E>}.\nThe Captain's Cabin, First Mate's Cabin, Helm, Survey Room, Crow's Nest, Masts and Deck cannot be changed to other rooms. Except for Cabins, Masts and Deck, a ship cannot have more than one room of the same type.\n\n

@61

@62

@63
Swap Ships (Shipyard > Dock)\nLeave ships at the dock or take them out.\nSailors, cargo and supplies can stay aboard while the ship is docked.\nWhen you take a ship out, you can also change the {Formation:<$41>} and {Crew Setup:<$8B>}.\n\n

@64

If you cancel, an ordinary sailor captains that ship. [!] Appointing the hero (the Admiral) as a ship's captain makes that ship the flagship.\n[!] A mate who becomes a captain levels up more easily.\n\n

@65

@66

You can change the policy from the Info command.\nEach fleet needs 100,000 gold coins in operating funds. You can form up to 6 fleets. If every ship in a fleet sinks, the mate returns to your fleet.\n> {Delegate Waters:<$43>}  > {Patrol:<$44>}  > {Call at Port:<$45>}  > {Attack Port:<$46>}\n\n

@67
Delegate Waters (Dock > Local Fleet)\nEntrust all activity in those waters to the fleet.\nThe mate who leads the local fleet decides on trade, investment and so on.\nOnce set, a monthly report of income and expenses arrives.\n\n

@68

@69
Call at Port (Dock > Local Fleet)\nCall at ports with a {contract:<$24>}.\n\n

@70
Attack Port (Dock > Local Fleet)\nAttack ports with no {contract:<$24>} to lower other factions' {Share:<$96>}.\n\n

@71
Edit Local Fleet (Shipyard > Dock)\nChange the makeup of a local fleet. Available only when the local fleet is docked at the same port.\n\n

@72
Disband Local Fleet (Shipyard > Dock)\nDisband a local fleet. Available only when the local fleet is docked at the same port.\nThe mate who led the fleet returns to the hero's fleet. Any remaining operating funds are returned too.\n\n

@86

@90

@91

@92

You capture the enemy flagship.\n[!] Beating an enemy fleet lowers the loser's {Share:<$96>} in ports near the battle. When you capture a ship, you may seize its gold coins and cargo.\n\n

@93
Victory Conditions: Ports\nYou defeat a port when either of these is met.\n1. The port's armament reaches 0.\n2. The port surrenders (it may surrender as its armament drops).\n[!] Winning a battle against a port gives you 10% of that port's {Share:<$96>}.\n\n

@94
Victory Conditions: Monsters\nYou win by defeating the monster. You may also get an item.\n\n

@95
